## 2. Moonbabies — Social Impact & Community Empowerment

### A. Mission & Philosophy
* **Core Focus:** Social movement, women empowerment, self-care education, human dignity, health, environmental responsibility, and community welfare.
* **Motto:** *"Glow with confidence, grow with kindness."* True glow comes not only from external skincare, but from kindness shared with the community.
* **Documentary Ethics Policy:** Adheres strictly to empathetic documentation standards—protecting the privacy, dignity, and autonomy of beneficiaries without patronizing or exploitative imagery.

### B. Program Activities & Formats
1. **Free Educational Workshops:** Self-care routines, basic skincare hygiene, mental wellness, and skill upgrading.
2. **Community Visits & Social Outreach:** Direct support for women groups, shelters, and marginalized communities.
3. **Environmental Campaigns:** Responsible packaging awareness, recycling initiatives, and green living practices.
4. **Wellness Activities:** Yoga, outdoor self-care, and holistic health workshops.
5. **Product Sharing Initiatives:** Donating hygiene and personal care packs to communities in need.

### C. Membership Benefits

* Networking with positive, supportive female communities across Indonesia.

## 4. Official Distributor & Reseller Partnership Program

### A. Business Proposition
* Expand Beautyinu distribution to local retail stores, regional cosmetics shops, beauty salons, and authorized online merchant networks across all Indonesian provinces.
* Products with high turnover (fast-moving consumer goods), strong viral brand awareness, and 100% legal BPOM compliance.

### B. Strategic Support for Partners
1. **Attractive Wholesale Margins:** Tiered pricing structure allowing healthy profit margins at all volume levels.
2. **Tester Products & Display Kits:** Free tester units for walk-in customer trials.
3. **Turnkey Promotional Assets:** High-resolution product images, video footage, KOL endorsement clips, and product display banners ready for social media and physical store branding.
4. **Marketing Campaign Synergy:** National advertising campaigns (Meta Ads, TikTok Ads) driving traffic to regional authorized distributors.
5. **Annual Distributor Reward (Reward 2026):** Tiered business rewards including vehicle incentives, operational support, and international trips for top annual GMV contributors.
6. **Price Protection Policy (HET):** Strict enforcement of *Harga Eceran Tertinggi / Terendah* to prevent price dumping and ensure fair competition across all channels.

### C. Partner Requirements
* Active retail shop (physical cosmetic store) or verified online storefront (Shopee, Tokopedia, TikTok Shop, WhatsApp Commerce).
* Commitment to maintain standard storage conditions (cool, dry, away from direct sunlight).
* Compliance with Beautyinu brand identity and official pricing policies.

## 5. Birthday Treats — Customer Loyalty & Appreciation

### A. Concept
* Personalized annual gift program celebrating loyal customers on their special day.
* Reaffirming the brand identity as *"Your Bodycare Bestie"*.

### B. Eligibility & Terms
* Must have an active purchase history or follow `@beautyinu.id` on Instagram.
* Claim window: Valid within 7 calendar days before or after the customer's actual birth date.
* Verification: Official government ID (KTP / SIM / Student Card) showing matching birth date and name.
* Delivery: Customer covers nominal shipping fee; the product gift itself is 100% complimentary.

### C. Step-by-Step Claim Flow
1. Send direct message (DM) to Instagram `@beautyinu.id` with keyword: `Klaim Birthday Treats`.
2. Attach a clear photo of valid ID (sensitive data like NIK may be masked).
3. Beautyinu customer care team validates data within 24–48 hours.
4. Once verified, customer receives delivery tracking code for the surprise birthday care package.
