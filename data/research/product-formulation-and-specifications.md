## 1. Product Portfolio Architecture

Beautyinu's product line is formulated around a systematic 5-step daily bodycare routine:

```
[Step 1: Cleanse]       ──>  Kefir Collagen Soap Bar (60g) / Bright Glow Body Wash (250ml)
[Step 2: Hydrate & Prep]──>  Bright Glow Body Toner (Exfoliate & Refresh)
[Step 3: Nourish & Tone]──>  Bright Glow Body Lotion UV Filter (200g / 750ml Jumbo)
[Step 4: Supercharge]   ──>  Brightening Booster Gold Powder (25g / 100g)
[Step 5: Lock & Restore]──>  Brightening Body Cream Grape Scent (100g / 200g)
```
